Submission Rules & Strict Duplicate Policy

  • 2 Separate Sets Required: Each department must upload both Set A and Set B in the prescribed DOCX format.
  • 50 MCQs Total: 25 questions in Section A (Research Methodology) and 25 questions in Section B (Subject-Specific Core Topics).
  • Duplicate Detection Limit (≤ 10 Repeated Questions): The system verifies each question against the past two exam sessions (January 2026 & July 2026). If a paper contains more than 10 repeated questions, it will be automatically rejected with a detailed question-by-question match report.
  • Standard Automatic Naming: Approved uploads are automatically formatted and stored as [CAMPUS]_[SET]_[DEPT]_Jan27.docx (e.g., RMP_A_CSE_Jan27.docx).
